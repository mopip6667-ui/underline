Privacy Policy

EERA · Last updated 25 November 2026

EERA does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your device

  • The cards you save, your reading position in each book, your text-size and page-colour choices, and your daily-card setting.
  • These are stored only on your phone. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • The optional backup code is created on your device and goes only where you paste it.

Internet use

When you are online, the app downloads new books from our public library files hosted on GitHub Pages. These requests contain no personal information. GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily card, the reminder is scheduled on your phone. No notification service or server is involved.

Links to other sites

Book pages can link to libraries and public-domain sources (for example your public library, Hoopla, Open Library, Project Gutenberg and Standard Ebooks). Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Children

EERA does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.
