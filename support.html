How do I save a card?

Double-tap the card, or tap the bookmark on the right. Saved cards appear under Saved.

How do I read a full book?

Open Library, then Full books, or tap the book icon on any card. Complete books are public-domain classics; for newer books, the book page links to free library borrowing.

Where do new books come from?

New books are added every week and appear automatically when you are online.

Moving to a new phone

Tap ⋯, then Copy backup code. On the new phone, tap ⋯, then Restore from backup code.

Daily card

Tap ⋯ and turn on Daily card to get one card as a notification each day at a time you choose.
